# Set 1 — Online Grocery: Product Vision and Roles

## Product Vision

**Vision Statement:**
To build a seamless, reliable online grocery shopping platform that enables customers to browse fresh produce, pantry staples, and household essentials, place orders with flexible delivery windows, and receive real-time order tracking — all within a single, intuitive digital experience.

**Goals:**
- Enable customers to order groceries online with same-day or scheduled delivery.
- Provide real-time inventory visibility to prevent out-of-stock surprises.
- Support multiple payment methods and easy reordering.
- Deliver a mobile-first experience for busy shoppers.

**Target Users:**
- Busy professionals and families who prefer home delivery.
- Elderly or mobility-limited customers needing grocery access.
- Small local grocers expanding to online sales.

## Stakeholders & Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Key Interests |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Product Owner (PO) | Owns the product backlog, defines vision, prioritises stories, accepts completed work | ROI, customer satisfaction, time-to-market |
| Scrum Master (SM) | Facilitates Scrum events, removes impediments, coaches the team | Team velocity, process health, impediment resolution |
| Development Team | Designs, codes, tests, and delivers product increments | Technical quality, sustainable pace, clear requirements |
| QA / Test Engineer | Validates functionality, writes test cases, reports defects | Bug-free releases, test coverage, regression safety |
| UX/UI Designer | Designs intuitive interfaces, conducts usability testing | User experience, accessibility, design consistency |
| Customer Support | Handles order issues, refunds, and delivery complaints | Fast resolution, clear escalation paths, customer retention |
| Inventory / Warehouse Team | Manages stock levels, picking, packing, and dispatch | Accurate inventory, efficient fulfillment |
| Payment Provider | Integrates payment gateways, handles transactions | Secure payments, low fraud, settlement speed |
| Compliance / Legal | Ensures data privacy, food safety labeling, terms of service | Regulatory compliance, risk mitigation |

## Success Metrics

- Monthly active users (MAU) growth
- Order completion rate (cart → placed order)
- On-time delivery percentage
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) score
- Average order value (AOV)
