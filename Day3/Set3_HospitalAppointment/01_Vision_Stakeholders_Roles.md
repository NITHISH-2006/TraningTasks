# Set 3 — Hospital Appointment: Vision, Stakeholders, and Roles

## Product Vision

**Vision Statement:**
To build a reliable, accessible hospital appointment scheduling platform that enables patients to book, reschedule, and cancel appointments online, gives doctors and staff real-time schedule visibility, and reduces no-shows through automated reminders — improving patient access and clinic efficiency.

**Goals:**
- Enable 24/7 online appointment booking across departments and doctors.
- Reduce phone-based scheduling by 70% within 6 months.
- Reduce no-show rate with automated SMS/email reminders.
- Provide real-time doctor availability and wait-time visibility.
- Support multi-location hospital networks with centralized scheduling.

**Target Users:**
- Patients booking appointments (routine, specialist, emergency follow-up)
- Doctors managing their schedules and patient lists
- Receptionists / front-desk staff handling walk-ins and phone bookings
- Hospital administrators overseeing clinic operations and utilization
- Nursing staff preparing for patient visits

## Stakeholders & Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Key Interests |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Product Owner (PO) | Owns product backlog, defines vision, prioritises features, accepts stories | Patient access, clinic efficiency, adoption rate |
| Scrum Master (SM) | Facilitates Scrum events, removes impediments, coaches team | Team velocity, sprint predictability, impediment resolution |
| Development Team | Builds booking engine, doctor schedules, reminder system | Technical quality, clear requirements, sustainable pace |
| QA / Test Engineer | Validates booking flows, reminders, calendar sync | Bug-free releases, test coverage, HIPAA-compliant testing |
| UX/UI Designer | Designs patient booking flow, doctor dashboard, admin console | Accessibility, ease of use for elderly patients, mobile responsiveness |
| Doctors / Clinicians | Review schedules, confirm appointments, mark availability | Accurate schedule, reduced double-booking, patient context |
| Receptionists / Front Desk | Manage walk-ins, phone bookings, check-ins | Quick lookup, waitlist management, room assignment |
| Hospital Administrators | Monitor clinic utilization, staff schedules, revenue | Resource optimization, reporting, compliance |
| IT / Infrastructure | Hosting, security, EHR integration, backups | System uptime, data security, integration reliability |
| Compliance / Legal (HIPAA) | Ensures patient data privacy, audit trails, consent | Regulatory compliance, data protection, breach prevention |
| Patients | Book, reschedule, cancel appointments; receive reminders | Ease of booking, reminder timeliness, data privacy |

## Success Metrics

- Appointment booking conversion rate (visit → booked)
- No-show rate reduction
- Percentage of bookings made online vs. phone
- Patient satisfaction (CSAT) score
- Doctor schedule utilization %
- Average wait time for appointment

## Regulatory Context

- **HIPAA (US)** or equivalent local health data privacy law
- Patient consent for SMS/email reminders
- Audit trail of all appointment changes (who, what, when)
- Secure storage of patient health information (PHI)
